**Ayame** is a private osu! server hosted in the EU, with low ping for European players,
extra gameplay modes, and every supporter perk unlocked for free.

<h3 align="center">Getting Started</h3>

<div align="center">

**1.** Register at [ayame.lol/register](https://ayame.lol/register)

**2.** Download the client from [ayame.lol/download](https://ayame.lol/download)

**3.** Log in and play

<details>
<summary><sub>Prefer your own osu! client?</sub></summary>
<br>

Launch osu! with the `-devserver ayame.lol` flag, then log in with your Ayame account.

</details>
